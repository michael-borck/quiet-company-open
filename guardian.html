The Eightfold / 01

The Guardian

For the one who always has to save the group chat.

The Guardian sits the way a good friend stands: between you and the thing you are dreading. The mask is not a threat. It is a promise that someone is paying attention.

An original sculpt. No licensed characters, no borrowed likeness. Just the posture.

The practice

Name the thing you are protecting today. Then name the thing you are protecting it from. If they are the same thing, put the figure down and go outside.
