The Everyman

For the one who is fine, actually.

The Everyman is round, seated, and holding a mug. No powers. A chair, a warm drink, and a tolerance for Tuesdays. That is the joke, and also the point.

An original sculpt, loosely in the spirit of Budai. No licensed characters, no borrowed likeness.

The practice

Sit down. That is it. The mug is optional.
