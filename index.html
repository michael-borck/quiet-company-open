Why they listen

Programmers keep a rubber duck on the desk and explain their bug to it. Talking is slower than thinking, so by the time you have explained the problem properly you have usually found the answer. The duck only listened.

A Quiet Company buddy does the same job. Put it where you work, say the problem out loud, and thank it afterwards.

How they are made

Every design is original. The shapes were generated from code with AI assistance, then checked and chosen by a person. We print each figure to order in matte PLA on a small farm of Prusa and Ultimaker machines. It arrives in two pieces, and you put the head on yourself. Each one comes with a card and a QR code to the longer story.
